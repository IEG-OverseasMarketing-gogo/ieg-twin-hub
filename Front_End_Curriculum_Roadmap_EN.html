3-Layer Architecture

The same phased SKU logic as Back-End — with one deliberate difference in Layer 2, explained below.

LAYER 1

SW Simulator

PC/tablet-based 3D process simulation covering all 8 stations. A complete loop from parameter manipulation → defect generation → diagnosis → 6D spatial pattern reading at EDS, with no physical equipment required.

Lite — Standalone SW license
LAYER 2

Metrology Integration

Connects to real measurement instruments (four-point probe, ellipsometer, optical microscope, profilometer) so learners verify Layer 1 predictions against real samples. Full process tools (steppers, implanters, etch chambers) are outside training-lab budgets, so Layer 2 targets metrology, not process equipment — see the note below.

Standard — L1 + metrology bundle
LAYER 3

Automated Assessment · Certification

Auto-generates dashboard reading logs, Pass/Fail matrices, SPC control charts, and 6D pattern-recognition scores. SCORM LMS integration, with certification-body consulting included.

Full — L1 + L2 + L3 + certification linkage

Why Layer 2 differs from Back-End: a wire bonder or die bonder costs roughly the same as a well-equipped classroom and is realistic for a training lab to own. A lithography stepper or ion implanter costs tens of millions of dollars — no TVET partner can own one for training purposes. Front-End's Layer 2 is scoped honestly around what a training lab can actually afford: real metrology instruments reading real (pre-processed) sample wafers, not real process chambers.

Standards Alignment

Front-End has not yet undergone a formal gap audit against a specific accreditation framework (unlike Back-End's BTEC mapping). The points below describe how the curriculum is structured to align with common TVET semiconductor standards — treat as a starting position for a partner-specific mapping exercise, not a completed audit.

Dashboard Reading & SPC Literacy

Every module's parameters are framed as Centerline/Control-Limit pairs, directly transferable to real SPC control-chart interpretation — see the companion Reading the Digital Twin instructor guide.

6D Spatial Pattern Intelligence

EDS's Wafer Map Pattern Drill is a distinctive competency not covered in most standard curricula — a candidate for a unique evidence artifact in any accreditation mapping.

Predictive Maintenance Literacy

12 of 13 front-end and back-end anomaly scenarios are modeled as gradual drifts with a named real-world sensor equivalent — see Appendix B of the Reading the Digital Twin guide.

Metrology Instrument Competency

Layer 2 gives hands-on time on four-point probes, ellipsometers, and optical microscopes — instruments common to most accreditation frameworks' measurement-competency units.

Evidence Formatting

Layer 3's automated reports (dashboard logs, Pass/Fail matrices, SPC charts) are designed to be reformatted to match whichever accreditation body's evidence template a partner requires.

Capstone Integration

The Wafer to Package Capstone (13-station root-cause tracing) is the natural evidence artifact for any "integrated project" or "Aim D"-equivalent unit requirement.

Three Commercial Packages

Mirrors the Back-End package structure for a consistent partner offer across the full 13-station curriculum.
