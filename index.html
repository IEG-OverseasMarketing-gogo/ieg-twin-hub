The 6D Framework
3DGeometry: a spatial model of the equipment, wafer, die and package.
4DTime: the process progresses step by step, such as cure cycles and bonding sequence.
5DData: dashboard indicators and anomaly alerts, with spec limits for each module.
6DSpatial pattern: Edge, Center, Donut, Scratch, Random and Local Cluster patterns on the wafer or package map.
The Diagnostic Loop Learners Practise
Read the patternIdentify the shape on the wafer or package map: Edge, Center, Donut, Scratch, Random or Local Cluster.
Predict the faultUse each guide's 6D Fault Case to list which stations could produce that shape.
Confirm at the stationCheck that station's own readings, such as temperature, pressure and yield, before naming the cause.
Shape narrows the diagnosis; data confirms it. Wire Bonding and Saw/Dicing deliberately give the same Scratch shape for different causes.
